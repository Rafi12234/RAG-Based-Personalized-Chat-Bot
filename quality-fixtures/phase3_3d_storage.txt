DocMind-AI QA fixture: Infrastructure

RustFS is the S3-compatible object store that holds uploaded source documents.
MySQL stores organizations, memberships, document metadata, and ingestion-job records.
Redis is used by the background task queue for ingestion dispatch.
Qdrant stores document vectors, not the original PDF or DOCX binary files.
